## Documents Needed

Keep these on hand when entering the terminal. Do **not** pack them in checked luggage.

- Boarding pass
- Luggage tags
- Passport or proof of citizenship documents
- Government-issued physical photo ID
- Any required visas or travel documents

Notes from Carnival boarding pass:

- Guests 16+ must present a **physical photo ID** such as driver’s license or school ID.
- Digital IDs are not accepted.
- Hospital-issued “footprint” birth certificates are not accepted.

## Safety and Policy Highlights

- All speakers, radios, and Bluetooth speakers are prohibited and may be confiscated.
- Use headphones/earbuds when listening to music or watching movies in public spaces.
- Marijuana, cannabis derivatives, and illegal substances are prohibited onboard and ashore.
- Smoking is only permitted in designated smoking areas.
- Do not throw anything overboard. Carnival notes this can result in a $500 charge per violation.
- Youth curfew: guests 17 and under must be out of public areas by 1:00 AM unless accompanied by an adult age 21+ from their traveling party.
- Be respectful in hallways and guest corridors; avoid running, shouting, or making noise near staterooms.
