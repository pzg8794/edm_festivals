## Aruba — June 30, 2026

**Port time:** 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM  
**Suggested back-near-ship time:** 9:30 PM latest

### Option 1: Self-guided downtown loop + Eagle Beach

A flexible, low-stress option.

#### Downtown Oranjestad walking loop

Estimated:

- About 2 miles total
- About 2 hours, not including food/drink/shopping stops

Stops:

1. **Plaza Daniel Leo** — colorful Dutch-style buildings near the terminal.
2. **Fort Zoutman** — Aruba’s oldest building and old lighthouse tower.
3. **City Hall** — green and white historic mansion.
4. **National Archaeological Museum** — Aruba history/culture stop.
5. **Queen Wilhelmina Park** — waterfront park, iguanas, photos, and shade.

#### Beach

- **Eagle Beach**
- Around 10–15 minutes by taxi from downtown/port area.

#### Food and drink possibilities

- Pastechis from local bakeries/vendors
- The West Deck — waterfront drinks/snacks
- Cuba’s Cookin’ — mojitos + Cuban food
- PAPPA’s Cigar Lounge — whiskey/rum vibes
- Yemanja Woodfired Grill — good for meat eaters and vegans
- CAYA House — trendy outdoor Caribbean-Latin spot

#### Notes

- Aruba tap water is known for being clean; bring a reusable water bottle.
- This plan lets people split off easily.
- Good balance of photos, culture, food, beach, and flexibility.

## Curaçao — July 2, 2026

**Port time:** 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
**Suggested back-near-ship time:** 4:00 PM latest

### Best current option: DIY Willemstad walking tour

Because the port day is shorter, stay close and keep a good buffer.

Potential stops:

- Queen Emma Bridge
- Colorful Willemstad waterfront buildings
- shops
- cafés
- local food
- photo spots

Food to try:

- Keshi Yena
- Funchi
- seafood

### Guided walking tour concern

A 3-hour walking tour starting at 1:30 PM would likely end around 4:30 PM. Since the ship departs at 5:00 PM, that is too tight unless the tour is cruise-sponsored or guarantees timely return.

### Carnival excursion idea from ship flyer

- **Snorkeling Adventure with Grilled Lunch & Open Bar** — double-decker catamaran, Curaçao coastline, sunken tugboat snorkel, grilled lunch, unlimited drinks.

## General Port Rules

- Ship time is law.
- Do not cut return times close.
- Carry cash + card.
- Stay in groups when drinking.
- Hydrate heavily.
- Bring ID, Sail & Sign card, sunscreen, portable charger, and water.
- Keep cruise documents and passport/ID secure.
