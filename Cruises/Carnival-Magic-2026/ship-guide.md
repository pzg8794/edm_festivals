## Quick Late-Night Food Advice

If eating time has passed and you need something quick:

1. Go to **Deck 10 Aft / Lido Marketplace area**.
2. Check **Pizza Pirate / Pizzeria del Capitano**.
3. Check **Late Night Snack** when available.
4. Check soft serve/frozen yogurt stations if still open.

## Medical Center Services

- Call **911** for medical emergencies from any ship phone.
- Call **4444** for non-medical emergencies or to schedule an appointment.

Hours listed on flyer:

- Sea Days: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM
- Port Days: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
- Medical service fees apply.

## Carnival HUB App

Use the Carnival HUB app for:

- What’s Happening activity schedule
- maps
- food venue hours
- onboard chat
- Sail & Sign account review
- restaurant/dining info

Download/use onboard chat after sail away:

1. Turn airplane mode on.
2. Connect to Carnival Magic Wi-Fi.
3. Download the app if needed.
4. Open Carnival HUB app.

## Internet / Wi-Fi

- Connect to `CarnivalWiFi`.
- Browse to CarnivalWiFi.com or follow prompts.
- Internet plan required for general web access.
- Cellular roaming can be expensive at sea; keep phone on airplane mode.

## Smoking Areas

From the Good to Know flyer:

- Indoors: Hat Trick Casino, Deck 5 Mid, only for guests seated and actively playing.

## Safety / Behavior Policies

- Ship is a shared space.
- Keep noise down in hallways and guest corridors.
- Children must be supervised.
- Guests 17 and under must be out of public areas by 1:00 AM unless with an adult 21+ from their travel party.
- Speakers, radios, and Bluetooth speakers are prohibited.
- Use headphones for music/movies in public areas.
- Beverages are not allowed on dance floors.
- Clack/clap fans are not allowed in the nightclub or indoor dance floors.
- Marijuana/cannabis derivatives and illegal substances are prohibited.
- Smoking is limited to designated areas only.
- Throwing/discharging items overboard may result in a $500 charge per violation.
