## Important privacy note

Do not commit ticket PDFs, boarding passes, barcode images, confirmation numbers, ticket numbers, phone numbers, or hotel booking documents. Keep those in Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, United app, Insomniac app, email, or a password-protected folder only.

## Weather planning summary

Las Vegas is expected to be very hot during the day and warm at night. Plan for breathable daytime clothes, sun protection, and light festival layers for overnight sets. See [weather and clothing guide](docs/09-weather-and-clothing.md).
