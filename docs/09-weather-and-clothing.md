## Clothing rules for the whole trip

- Wear shoes you can stand and dance in for 8-10 hours.
- Avoid heavy black daytime outfits unless you stay indoors or shaded.
- Bring at least one light hoodie or overshirt for planes, shuttle air conditioning, and late-night wind.
- Use sunscreen before leaving the resort and reapply before shuttles.
- Bring one backup festival outfit in case of sweat, spills, or discomfort.

## Outfit packing count

- 2 travel outfits.
- 3 festival outfits, plus 1 backup.
- 2-3 resort or pool outfits.
- 5-6 pairs of socks.
- 1 pair of reliable festival sneakers.
- 1 pair of sandals or slides for resort only.
- 1 hat or cap.
- Sunglasses you are willing to lose.
