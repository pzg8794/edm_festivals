## Purpose

This note documents the final full day on the cruise so it can be referenced later for memories, planning, captions, or future cruise routines.

## Best recommendation

The best final-day flow was:

1. Brunch at Southern Lights.
2. Relax around Lido / pool / ocean-view areas.
3. Take final cruise photos.
4. Attend either comedy or America Rocks.
5. End with the Fourth of July Deck Party on Lido.
6. Pack before midnight.

This gives a strong final memory without running around too much.

## Disembarkation preparation checklist

Before going to sleep:

- Pack most clothes and souvenirs.
- Keep out the next morning’s outfit.
- Keep passport / ID, Sail & Sign card, phone, charger, medications, and toiletries accessible.
- Check final Sail & Sign account charges.
- If using luggage pickup, place bags outside by the required time.
- Do not pack anything needed in the morning.
- Keep valuables and travel documents in the carry-on.

## Personal reminder

Do not leave packing until after midnight. Enjoy the final night, but protect tomorrow morning from chaos.

The priority is: **final memories + easy disembarkation**.
