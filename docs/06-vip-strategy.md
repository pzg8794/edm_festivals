# VIP Strategy

## Best ways to use VIP

- Use VIP viewing when you are committed to a stage for at least 45-60 minutes.
- Use Kinetic Field VIP heavily on Sunday because Zedd, Martin Garrix, and Armin are all there.
- Use VIP bathrooms proactively before major set transitions.
- Use VIP charging lockers only as backup; still carry your own power bank.
- Use Beats & Breakfast during sunrise windows instead of buying extra food inside the festival.
- Do not over-walk just to use VIP areas; stage conflicts can burn more energy than they are worth.

## Stage priority by your taste

1. Kinetic Field: big-room, mainstage, pop-EDM, best VIP value.
2. Circuit Grounds: Tiesto and large-scale electronic sets.
3. Quantum Valley: trance and melodic late-night routes.
4. Cosmic Meadow: strong house picks and VIP dance area.

## Night-by-night VIP plan

- Friday: try Cosmic Meadow or Quantum Valley VIP, but do not over-commit until you know the layout.
- Saturday: use Circuit Grounds VIP for Tiesto; move to Kinetic VIP for Kaskade and Above & Beyond.
- Sunday: stay at Kinetic VIP from Zedd through Armin unless you need a reset.
